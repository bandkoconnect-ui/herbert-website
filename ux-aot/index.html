Accenture Operations Twin

Overview and manage entire production facilities down to the smallest detail through their virtual twin with the use of AI and VR.

Challenge

Accenture saw the huge potential in combining digital twins with advanced predictive AI. To bring this vision to life, it teamed up with global industry leaders to create its own Digital Twin Operator—a tool that gives a complete, real-time view of every aspect of production. Designed for everyone from plant-floor workers to top executives, it uses smart access management and predictive AI to make sure the right people get the right information exactly when they need it.

Partners and suppliers

Journeys and prototypes

Research

My team ran in-depth interviews and multiple workshops to uncover both clear and hidden requirements. We used those insights to build prototypes and a first draft, shaping how the product would look, feel, and function.

Flexibility in the design

Design

We designed the system with extensive customization options to accommodate a wide range of users, from factory floor operators to C-level executives.

Making a robust
design system

The Accenture Operations Twin will share its design system with other products in the same family, ensuring a consistent experience across the ecosystem.

Extra bit

A global project

The Accenture Operations Twin brought together teams from around the world and across different parts of Accenture. It was a huge collaborative effort, carried out under the uncertainty and pressure of the COVID-19 pandemic, to successfully deliver across the multiple design streams.
